# Subject-Led Studio

A named design philosophy, not the default frontend workflow. General UI work belongs to `impeccable`; this skill supplies an aesthetic lens when requested.

- Derive visual identity from the subject's real materials, artifacts, audience, and vocabulary. The brief and existing design system take precedence; do not invent product facts.
- Give the surface one characteristic element, with quieter supporting structure. A hero can be a headline, artifact, demo, or interaction rather than a fixed template.
- Let typography carry personality through deliberate display/body roles, scale, weight, and spacing. Complexity should fit the direction, not demonstrate effort.
- Structural devices carry meaning: number actual sequences, not unrelated cards. Motion should serve the subject rather than decorate every transition.
- Use recognizable user-facing nouns and specific action verbs. Keep action names consistent; errors and empty states explain the next available action.

Preserve mobile usability, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion preferences. Aesthetic risk does not justify inaccessible controls or unsupported claims.
